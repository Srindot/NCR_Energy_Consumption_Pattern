Temperature Projections for Delhi NCR (2025-2044)
============================================================

METHODOLOGY
------------------------------------------------------------
Baseline Year: 2024
Baseline Temperature: 25.5°C
Projection Period: 2025 to 2044

SCENARIOS
------------------------------------------------------------

Conservative (SSP1-2.6)
  Rate: +0.4°C per decade
  Description: Low emissions scenario with strong climate action
  Source: IPCC AR6 SSP1-2.6 scenario for South Asia

Moderate (SSP2-4.5)
  Rate: +0.6°C per decade
  Description: Middle-of-the-road scenario with moderate emissions reduction
  Source: IPCC AR6 SSP2-4.5 scenario for South Asia

High (SSP5-8.5)
  Rate: +1.0°C per decade
  Description: High emissions scenario with limited climate action
  Source: IPCC AR6 SSP5-8.5 scenario for South Asia


CITATION
------------------------------------------------------------
Temperature projections based on:
- IPCC (2021). Climate Change 2021: The Physical Science Basis.
  Sixth Assessment Report, Working Group I. Chapter 12: South Asia.
- India Meteorological Department (IMD). Climate Scenarios for India.
- Ministry of Earth Sciences (2020). Assessment of Climate Change
  over the Indian Region.

Note: Projections include small random variations (±0.1°C) to
represent natural climate variability.
